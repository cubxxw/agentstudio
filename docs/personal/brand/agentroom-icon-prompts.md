# AgentRoom icon generation prompts

Tool: built-in imagegen. Both calls requested real transparency. The raw final output is preserved; native export masking is handled separately by `dev/export-brand-icons.swift`.

## Selected direction

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original macOS app icon for AgentRoom, a personal room for your chats and AI agents.
Invent a very distinctive original ugly-cute designer-toy mascot called "the odd roommate": a short, squat dark forest-green doorway creature, asymmetrical and mischievous, with an instantly readable silhouette. The creature's body is one chunky softened inverted-U arch like a little room entrance, with a slightly lopsided almost flat top and no roof or chimney. Its forehead has a single small off-center rounded bump, NOT ears. Two large warm ivory eyes are embedded in the arch's thick upper crossbar; eyes at subtly different heights, little dark pupils glancing sideways, one droopy upper lid; a memorable deadpan suspicious expression. The lower inner arch is a near-black negative-space doorway which doubles as its open smirk, with exactly one broad crooked ivory front tooth dropping from the arch's upper inner edge, slightly off-center. Its lower ends form two squat stumpy feet, integrated into the arch shape. Large simple volumes, no arms, no additional decorations. It looks like a tiny weird roommate who is competent but up to something.
Premium minimal sculptural art-toy app icon, softly rounded matte molded vinyl with subtle tactile material, not fluffy. Deep forest-green body, warm ivory eyes/tooth, espresso negative space. Pale muted pistachio/cream rounded-square background tile with elegant broad safe margins and transparent outer canvas. Square composition, one centered standalone icon. Tile fills 86 percent of canvas; character fills 72 percent of tile. Straight-on view with a tiny visible top plane and subtle soft upper-left shadows. Strong dark silhouette and high eye contrast at 32 px. Creature should feel a bit awkward, iconic, knowing and collectible, never babyish.
No text, no wordmark, no letters, no labels, no collage. No long ears, no rabbit or animal suit, no existing character identity, no Labubu copying, no many-toothed smile, no fur, no nose, no rosy cheeks, no sparkles, no gradients in blue or purple. One app icon only.
```

## Final presentation edit

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Edit target: the supplied green AgentRoom app icon.
Make this the final production version of the SAME approved icon. Preserve the original green arch creature's silhouette, exact facial personality, asymmetric eyes, one off-center ivory tooth, doorway mouth, forest-green matte vinyl material, pale pistachio tile, camera angle, lighting and overall arrangement. Do not redesign the character.
Change ONLY the native app icon presentation:
1. Make the background tile a geometrically clean, smooth rounded square with equal precise corner radii.
2. Leave a consistent completely transparent outer margin on all four sides, approximately 10 percent of the total square canvas.
3. Outside the rounded-square tile and its very delicate natural shadow, every pixel must be fully transparent. Remove all scattered edge specks, white scraps, ghost texture and stray opaque pixels around the outside perimeter.
4. Keep the mascot comfortably within the rounded-square tile with clear visual breathing room; preserve all facial features.
Deliver one single centered SQUARE app icon, clean high-quality 1024x1024 PNG with real alpha transparency beyond the tile. No captions, text, frames, new elements, collage or additional variants. The result must remain unmistakably the same approved design.
```
